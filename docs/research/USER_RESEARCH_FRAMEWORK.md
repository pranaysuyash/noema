## 1. Research Objectives

### Pre-Launch Research (Months 1-3)

**Primary Objectives:**
1. Validate core value proposition (emotional intelligence + note-taking)
2. Understand emotional privacy concerns and boundaries
3. Identify optimal mood tracking frequency and UI
4. Test gamification mechanics for engagement vs. manipulation perception
5. Assess willingness to pay for premium features

**Research Questions:**
- Do users find value in mood-based note organization?
- What privacy concerns exist around emotional data?
- How do users currently track mood and notes (if at all)?
- What would make them switch from current tools?
- Is the freemium pricing appropriate?

### Beta Testing (Months 4-5)

**Primary Objectives:**
1. Validate AI mood detection accuracy
2. Test cloud sync reliability
3. Measure engagement with gamification features
4. Identify usability issues in core flows
5. Assess performance on target devices (iPhone 13+)

## 3. Research Methodology

### Phase 1: Discovery (Month 1)

**Methods:**
1. **User Interviews (N=50)**
   - Duration: 45 minutes
   - Compensation: $50 Amazon gift card
   - Format: Video call (Zoom)
   - Focus: Current tools, pain points, privacy concerns

2. **Competitive Analysis**
   - Test Notion, Saner.AI, Day One, Daylio
   - Document features, pricing, UX
   - Identify gaps

3. **Surveys (N=500)**
   - Quant data on demographics, willingness to pay
   - Tool: Google Forms or Typeform
   - Distribution: Reddit, Twitter, ProductHunt

### Phase 2: Concept Testing (Month 2-3)

**Methods:**
1. **Prototype Testing (N=30)**
   - Tool: Figma prototypes
   - Tasks: Create note, view mood timeline, unlock achievement
   - Metrics: Task completion rate, time on task, errors

2. **Pricing Research (N=200)**
   - Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Meter
   - Questions:
     - At what price would you consider it too expensive?
     - At what price would you consider it a bargain?
     - At what price does it start to get expensive?
     - At what price would you consider it too cheap to be good quality?

3. **A/B Testing**
   - Test variations:
     - Mood detection: Auto vs. Manual default
     - Gamification: Visible vs. Subtle
     - Pricing: $9.99 vs. $7.99 vs. $12.99

### Phase 3: Beta Testing (Month 4-5)

**Methods:**
1. **Closed Beta (N=100 → 5,000)**
   - Wave 1: 100 super fans
   - Wave 2: 500 users
   - Wave 3: 2,000 users
   - Wave 4: 5,000 users

2. **In-App Feedback**
   - NPS survey after 7 days
   - Feature satisfaction after each use
   - Bug reporting (shake to report)

3. **Usage Analytics**
   - Amplitude: Track feature usage, retention
   - Crashlytics: Monitor stability
   - TestFlight: Collect crash reports

## 4. Beta Testing Program

### Recruitment Criteria

**Must Have:**
- iPhone 13 or newer
- iOS 17+
- Active note-taker (3+ notes/week)
- Interested in wellness/self-improvement
- Willing to provide feedback

**Nice to Have:**
- Current therapy patient
- Uses productivity apps (Notion, Evernote)
- Uses wellness apps (Headspace, Calm)
- Active on social media (potential advocates)

### Beta Program Structure

**Week 1: Onboarding**
- Welcome email with TestFlight link
- Onboarding survey: Demographics, expectations
- Discord invite for community

**Week 2-3: Core Features**
- Focus: Note creation, mood tracking
- Survey: NPS, feature satisfaction

**Week 4-5: Advanced Features**
- Focus: Cloud sync, gamification, integrations
- Survey: Privacy perception, willingness to pay

**Week 6: Wrap-Up**
- Exit interview (N=30 selected users)
- Final survey: Overall satisfaction, launch readiness
- Testimonial collection for App Store

### Beta Tester Incentives

- **Participation**: 3 months free Pro (post-launch)
- **Active Feedback**: 6 months free Pro
- **Top Contributors**: 12 months free Pro + swag
- **Referrals**: +1 month Pro per successful referral

## 5. User Interview Guide

### Pre-Interview (5 min)

1. **Consent & Recording**
   - "Is it okay if I record this session for note-taking purposes?"
   - "Your responses will be anonymous and used only for product development"

2. **Set Expectations**
   - "This is a conversation, not a test. There are no right or wrong answers"
   - "We're here to learn about your experiences, not sell you anything"

### Background & Context (10 min)

1. **Current Tools**
   - "What apps or tools do you currently use for note-taking?"
   - "How do you track your mood or emotional state?"
   - "What do you like about your current tools? What frustrates you?"

2. **Workflows**
   - "Walk me through the last time you created a note. What was the context?"
   - "How often do you revisit old notes? What are you looking for?"

3. **Pain Points**
   - "What's the biggest challenge you face with your current note-taking system?"
   - "Have you ever wanted to find a note based on how you were feeling?"

### noema Concept Introduction (15 min)

1. **Pitch**
   - Show: 30-second demo video
   - "noema is an AI note-taking app that understands not just what you're saying, but how you're feeling"

2. **Initial Reaction**
   - "What's your first reaction to this concept?"
   - "What excites you? What concerns you?"

3. **Feature Walkthrough**
   - Demo: Mood detection, timeline, gamification
   - "Which of these features is most valuable to you?"
   - "Which feels unnecessary or gimmicky?"

### Privacy & Trust (10 min)

1. **Privacy Concerns**
   - "What concerns do you have about an app analyzing your emotional data?"
   - "Would you trust an app with your personal thoughts? What would make you trust it?"

2. **On-Device vs. Cloud**
   - "If I told you 95% of processing happens on your device, how does that affect your trust?"
   - "Would you opt-in to cloud AI for better features, or stick with on-device?"

3. **Data Ownership**
   - "How important is it that you can export or delete your data at any time?"

### Pricing & Willingness to Pay (5 min)

1. **Value Perception**
   - "Based on what you've seen, how much would you expect this app to cost per month?"
   - "What would you consider too expensive? Too cheap?"

2. **Freemium Model**
   - Show: Free vs. Pro tiers
   - "Would the free tier be useful to you, or would you need Pro?"

### Wrap-Up (5 min)

1. **Final Thoughts**
   - "If noema launched tomorrow, would you try it? Why or why not?"
   - "What would make this a must-have app for you?"

2. **Referrals**
   - "Do you know anyone else who might be interested in this concept?"

## 6. Usability Testing Protocol

### Test Setup

**Environment:**
- In-person or remote (Zoom with screen share)
- TestFlight build on participant's device
- Session recording (with consent)

**Materials:**
- Test script with tasks
- Pre-test questionnaire
- Post-test questionnaire (SUS, NPS)
- Note-taking template

### Test Tasks

**Task 1: Create a Text Note**
- Scenario: "You just had a great conversation with a friend. Capture the key points in a note."
- Success: Note created with content
- Metrics: Time to complete, errors, satisfaction (1-5)

**Task 2: Record a Voice Note**
- Scenario: "You're walking and have an idea. Record it as a voice note."
- Success: Audio recorded and transcribed
- Metrics: Time, transcription accuracy perceived, satisfaction

**Task 3: View Mood Timeline**
- Scenario: "You want to see how your mood has changed over the past week. Find that information."
- Success: Mood timeline viewed
- Metrics: Time, wrong taps, understanding

**Task 4: Unlock an Achievement**
- Scenario: "You've created 5 notes. See if you've unlocked any achievements."
- Success: Achievement viewed
- Metrics: Discoverability, delight level

**Task 5: Enable Cloud Sync**
- Scenario: "You want your notes on your iPad too. Enable cloud sync."
- Success: Cloud sync enabled
- Metrics: Clarity of privacy implications, trust level

### Observation Notes

- **Hesitations**: Where does user pause or seem confused?
- **Errors**: Taps on wrong element, backs out of flow
- **Verbalizations**: "I'm looking for...", "I expected..."
- **Emotional Reactions**: Delight, frustration, surprise
- **Workarounds**: User finds alternative path (good or bad?)

### Post-Task Questions

After each task:
1. "On a scale of 1-5, how easy was that task?"
2. "Was that what you expected to happen?"
3. "Anything you would change?"

### Post-Test Questionnaire

**System Usability Scale (SUS):**
1. I think I would like to use this app frequently (1-5)
2. I found the app unnecessarily complex (1-5)
3. I thought the app was easy to use (1-5)
4. I would need support to use this app (1-5)
5. The app's functions were well integrated (1-5)
6. There was too much inconsistency in this app (1-5)
7. Most people would learn this app quickly (1-5)
8. I found the app very cumbersome to use (1-5)
9. I felt very confident using the app (1-5)
10. I needed to learn a lot before I could use this app (1-5)

**SUS Score Calculation:**
- Odd questions: Subtract 1 from score
- Even questions: 5 minus score
- Sum all, multiply by 2.5
- **Target SUS: >80 (Excellent)**

## 7. Survey Templates

### NPS Survey (Day 7)

**Question:**
"How likely are you to recommend noema to a friend or colleague?"

[0-10 scale]

**Follow-up (based on score):**
- **Promoters (9-10)**: "What do you love most about noema?"
- **Passives (7-8)**: "What would make you love noema?"
- **Detractors (0-6)**: "What's the main reason for your score?"

### Feature Satisfaction Survey

After first use of each major feature:

**Mood Detection:**
"How satisfied are you with the mood detection feature?"
- [ ] Very satisfied
- [ ] Satisfied
- [ ] Neutral
- [ ] Dissatisfied
- [ ] Very dissatisfied

"How accurate was the mood detection?"
- [ ] Very accurate
- [ ] Mostly accurate
- [ ] Somewhat accurate
- [ ] Not accurate

"Any comments or suggestions?"
[Text box]

### Exit Survey (End of Beta)

1. **Overall Experience**
   - "How would you rate your overall experience with noema?" (1-5)

2. **Feature Usage**
   - "Which features did you use regularly?" [Checkboxes]
   - "Which features did you never use? Why?"

3. **Value Proposition**
   - "Did noema help you understand your emotional patterns better?" (Yes/No/Somewhat)
   - "Would you pay for noema?" (Yes/No)
   - "If yes, how much per month?" [$X.XX]

4. **Launch Readiness**
   - "Is noema ready to launch to the public?" (Yes/No)
   - "If no, what's missing?"

5. **Testimonial**
   - "May we use your feedback in our marketing? If yes, please write a brief testimonial:"

## 8. Success Metrics

### Research Phase Success Criteria

**User Interviews (Month 1):**
- ✅ Completed: 50 interviews
- ✅ Privacy concerns identified: 3 major themes
- ✅ Value prop validated: >70% express interest

**Prototype Testing (Month 2-3):**
- ✅ Task completion rate: >85%
- ✅ SUS Score: >75
- ✅ Mood detection perceived accuracy: >80%

**Beta Testing (Month 4-5):**
- ✅ NPS: >50
- ✅ Week 1 retention: >80%
- ✅ Day 30 retention: >50%
- ✅ Crash-free sessions: >99%
- ✅ Conversion intent: >5% would pay

### Launch Readiness Checklist

- [ ] NPS >50 from beta testers
- [ ] App Store rating (beta) >4.5
- [ ] <20 known P0/P1 bugs
- [ ] Privacy audit passed
- [ ] Performance benchmarks met
- [ ] 100+ testimonials collected
- [ ] Pricing validated (acceptable range identified)
- [ ] Core flows SUS >80
