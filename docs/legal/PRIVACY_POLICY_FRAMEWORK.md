## Our Privacy Commitment

**Privacy is not a feature at noema—it's a fundamental right.**

We built noema from the ground up with privacy as the foundation, not an afterthought. Your thoughts, emotions, and personal reflections belong to you, and only you.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

**Account Information:**
- Email address (via Sign in with Apple)
- Display name (optional)
- Profile picture (optional)

**User Content:**
- Note text content
- Audio recordings (voice notes)
- Mood logs and emotional data
- Tags and organizational metadata
- Achievements and gamification progress

**Optional Integrations (User-Controlled):**
- HealthKit data (sleep, exercise, heart rate)
- Calendar events (meeting times only, not content)
- Apple Music listening data

### 1.2 Information Automatically Collected

**Device Information:**
- Device model and iOS version
- App version
- Language and region settings

**Usage Data (Anonymous):**
- Feature usage patterns
- App performance metrics
- Crash reports (anonymous)

**Location Data (City-Level Only):**
- City name for weather data (GPS coordinates stored locally only)

### 1.3 Information We DO NOT Collect

❌ We DO NOT collect:
- SMS or call logs
- Contacts
- Precise GPS coordinates (for cloud storage)
- Browsing history
- Data from other apps
- Biometric data (Face ID/Touch ID processed on-device only)

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

### 2.1 Core App Functionality

- **Note Storage**: Store and sync your notes securely
- **AI Processing**: Analyze text for mood detection, summarization (on-device by default)
- **Gamification**: Track achievements, streaks, and progress
- **Cloud Sync**: Sync encrypted notes across your devices (opt-in)

### 2.2 Product Improvement (Opt-In Only)

- **Anonymous Analytics**: Understand feature usage to improve the app
- **Crash Reporting**: Fix bugs and improve stability
- **A/B Testing**: Test new features with user groups

### 2.3 Communications

- **Transactional**: Account-related emails (password reset, subscription updates)
- **Notifications**: Streak reminders, achievement unlocks (opt-in)
- **Marketing**: Product updates and tips (opt-in, easy unsubscribe)

---

## 3. How We Protect Your Information

### 3.1 Encryption at Rest

- **Local Storage**: AES-256 encryption using iOS Data Protection API
- **Cloud Storage**: End-to-end encryption (E2EE) with user-controlled keys
- **Zero-Knowledge**: We cannot decrypt your notes even if compelled

### 3.2 Encryption in Transit

- **TLS 1.3**: All network communication over HTTPS
- **Certificate Pinning**: Protection against man-in-the-middle attacks

### 3.3 On-Device AI Processing

- **95% Local**: Most AI processing happens on your device
- **No Server Logs**: On-device processing leaves no server traces
- **Optional Cloud AI**: Advanced features require explicit opt-in

### 3.4 Access Controls

- **Biometric Authentication**: Face ID/Touch ID required to unlock app
- **Privacy Mode**: Hide content in app switcher
- **Selective Sync**: Choose which notes sync to cloud

---

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 4.1 We DO NOT Sell Your Data

**Period. Never. Ever.**

Your personal data, especially emotional data, is not a commodity.

### 4.2 Service Providers (Minimal)

We share data only with essential service providers:

| Provider | Purpose | Data Shared | Safeguards |
|----------|---------|-------------|------------|
| Apple CloudKit | Cloud sync | Encrypted notes | E2EE, Apple's privacy policy |
| OpenAI | Cloud AI (opt-in) | Note text (encrypted in transit) | No training on user data, ephemeral processing |
| Stripe | Payment processing | Email, payment method | PCI-DSS compliant |
| Amplitude | Analytics (opt-in) | Anonymous usage data | No PII, aggregated only |
| Firebase | Crash reporting (opt-in) | Anonymous crash logs | No user content |

### 4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose data if required by law:
- Valid subpoena or court order
- Comply with legal obligations
- Protect rights, property, or safety

**Our Commitment:**
- We will notify you unless prohibited
- We will challenge overly broad requests
- We publish a transparency report annually

### 4.4 Business Transfers

If noema is acquired or merged:
- This privacy policy remains in effect
- You will be notified of any material changes
- You can delete your account before transfer

## 8. Data Retention

### 8.1 Active Accounts

- **Notes**: Retained indefinitely until you delete them
- **Moods**: Retained indefinitely until you delete them
- **Achievements**: Retained indefinitely

### 8.2 Deleted Accounts

- **Immediate**: Account disabled, access revoked
- **30 Days**: Grace period for recovery
- **After 30 Days**: Permanent deletion from all systems
- **Backups**: Deleted from backups within 90 days

### 8.3 Anonymous Data

- **Usage Analytics**: 2 years
- **Crash Reports**: 90 days
- **Aggregated Insights**: Indefinitely (cannot be linked back to you)

## 11. Security Practices

### 11.1 Incident Response

If a security breach occurs:
1. **Immediate**: Investigate and contain
2. **Within 72 Hours**: Notify affected users (GDPR)
3. **Within 30 Days**: Public disclosure (if material)

### 11.2 Security Audits

- **Quarterly**: Internal security reviews
- **Annually**: Third-party security audits
- **Bug Bounty**: $10K reward for critical vulnerabilities

### 11.3 Employee Access

- **Zero Access**: Employees cannot access encrypted user data
- **Least Privilege**: Access only to what's needed for job
- **Audit Logs**: All access is logged and reviewed
